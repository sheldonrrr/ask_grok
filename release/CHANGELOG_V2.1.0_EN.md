## GitHub Release Notes (v2.1.0)

### Changes
- Read an EPUB inside the plugin. Select a passage, add a note, and ask with the AI you already configured. The reply streams in beside the page. Open it with Ctrl+Shift+V (Cmd+Shift+V on macOS). Calibre’s View stays on V.
- While a reply is on the way, the pane shows a short “requesting” line. If you stop the request or it fails, the note you typed comes back. A finished reply does not leave that note in the box.
- Change the model, font size, line spacing, and a light, sepia, or night page while you read. On a night page the body text turns light as well as the headings. The same size and spacing apply to the reply.
- Pictures are not shown. A chapter that is only pictures is skipped. A picture inside a chapter becomes a short placeholder with its name.
- Reading questions have their own prompt settings under Configuration → Prompts. Choose whether to include the book, the chapter, nearby text, the selection, and your note. Edit the task wording, how many characters of nearby text to send, and the default note. A preview uses a short passage from Frankenstein. The older title-and-question templates are left as they were.
- The reader remembers the chapter, how far you had scrolled, the window size, and whether the window was full screen or maximized. The next time you open that book, it returns there.
- History in the reader lists earlier questions for that book, including ones from the Ask window. Opening a saved answer starts at the beginning of the reply.
